Screenshots

Visual overview of key features and functionality

1

Setup Multi Currency

Configure and setup multiple currencies for your POS system. Go to Accounting > Configuration > Currencies to select and enable currencies for your point of sale operations.

2

Configure POS Payment Methods Currency Wise

Configure payment methods with specific currencies. Go to Point of Sale > Configuration > Payment Method with Currency to assign each payment method to its corresponding currency with proper journal for proper multi-currency cash control.

3

Multi-Currency Opening Balance

POS opening balance popup with multiple currency inputs (USD, EUR, AED, etc.). Set opening cash amount for each currency individually when starting a POS session. The opening note section displays the total opening balance across all currencies.

4

Multi-Currency Cash In/Out

Cash In/Out dialog with multi-currency support. Perform cash movements in any configured currency (USD, EUR, AED, etc.). Select the currency from the dropdown, enter the amount, and provide a reason for the transaction. Track all cash movements currency-wise with complete audit trail.

5

Multi-Currency Payment Processing

Process payments in multiple currencies with real-time currency conversion. Each payment line displays both the foreign currency amount (EUR, AED, etc.) and its equivalent in base currency (USD). The system automatically calculates and displays the total amount across all currencies. Select payment methods from different currencies to split payments or pay in foreign currency.

6

Receipt with Multi-Currency Details

POS receipt displaying complete payment information in multiple currencies. The receipt shows each payment method with its currency amount and the equivalent in base currency, providing clear visibility of multi-currency transactions for customers and accounting records.

7

Multi-Currency Closing Balance

Closing register dialog with comprehensive multi-currency cash control. View currency exchange rates, starting balances, expected ending balances, and cash in/out movements for each currency (USD, EUR, AED, etc.). The system calculates differences between expected and counted amounts, displays all currency-wise operations including payments and cash movements, and provides a total cash count in base currency.

8

POS Session Backend Tracking

Complete backend tracking of POS sessions with multi-currency support. View detailed session information including opening and closing balances for each currency (USD, EUR, AED, etc.), total amounts in base currency, and a comprehensive activity log showing all currency-wise operations. Track cash movements, payments, and all transactions with complete audit trail and real-time currency conversion totals.

9

Multi-Currency Journal Entries

Automatic accounting journal entries for all multi-currency POS transactions. Each transaction is recorded with both the original currency amount (USD, EUR, AED, etc.) and the equivalent in base currency. The system automatically creates proper accounting entries showing debit and credit amounts, tax calculations, sales accounts, accounts receivable, and cash accounts for each currency. Complete double-entry bookkeeping with currency conversion ensures accurate financial records and full audit trail for multi-currency operations.
